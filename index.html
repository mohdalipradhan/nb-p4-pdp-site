Wall check

Know your wall before you mount.

The wall matters as much as the TV. Check the wall material and use fixings suited to it.

SOLID MASONRY · BRICK / CONCRETE

Confirm the right fixings.

Sound solid brick or concrete gives suitable anchors material to grip. Check your wall and use fixings specified for it.

HOLLOW / GYPSUM · ASK FIRST

Ask an installer first.

Hollow block, gypsum and mortar joints need a different fixing plan. Do not rely on an anchor ending in an empty cavity.

FAQ
NB P4 Questions, Answered
1. Will it fit my TV?

If your TV is 32–55", under 27.2 kg and has VESA holes between 100×100 and 400×400 mm, yes. Not sure? WhatsApp your TV model to +91 91319 56290 before ordering.

2. Can I use it with a 60" or larger TV?

No. The P4 is designed for 32–55" TVs up to 27.2 kg. Ask us about a bigger mount.

3. Will my TV sag when pulled out?

All extending arms carry more leverage when extended. Stay within 27.2 kg, tighten the joints and fold back when not angling. ±4° levelling helps keep it straight.

4. What kind of wall do I need?

Solid brick or concrete is best. Avoid hollow blocks, plaster-only spots and mortar joints, and use every anchor point.

5. Are the TV screws included?

A hardware kit with wall and TV screws is in the box. Some TVs need a specific screw length. WhatsApp us your model.

6. Can I mount it in a corner?

Yes, if the wall behind the plate is solid and there’s room for the TV to swivel.
